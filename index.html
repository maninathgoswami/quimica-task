About Us

Office Location · Chemical Engineering Dept.

Quimica is the official society of the Chemical Engineering Department at B.I.T Sindri. We bring together students who are as passionate about thermodynamics at 2am as they are about the chaos of fest season.

From technical webinars to full-scale annual fests, we organize events that push students beyond textbooks — into real problem-solving, peer learning, and engineering culture.
